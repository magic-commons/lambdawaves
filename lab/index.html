WHAT IS EXACT, WHAT IS NOT

EXACT ANALYTIC — the 91 states n ≤ 6 are closed-form Rnl(r)Ylm(θ,φ) (complex Y, Condon–Shortley phase, atomic units); En = −1/(2n²); field-free evolution is ca(t) = e−iEatca(0), a pure phase per mode. Norm, ⟨E⟩ and the autocorrelation are computed from these coefficients, not from the grid.

EXACT REAL REPRESENTATION — SHADOW reads the same c(t) as q = √2 Re c, p = √2 Im c. For diagonal H that is exactly a set of uncoupled harmonic oscillators, and HC = ⟨H⟩. It is an equation-level identity, not a claim about ontology.

NUMERICAL — FIELD is ψ sampled on an N³ grid (f16), trilinearly filtered and ray-marched. Voxel values agree with the closed form to ~10−5; the picture is limited by grid resolution and the transfer function, never by the physics. Quality settings change only this estimate.

TRUNCATED / MASKED — muting or soloing removes modes from the reconstruction only; the state keeps them and the badge says what fraction of the norm you are looking at. A finite basis (n ≤ 6) is not the whole atom.

Camera orbit, slice and clip are observer operations. STATE ROTATE, STARK ROTATE (e−iθKz, an SO(4) rotation) and DEFECT WAIT (eiαL², not one) are physical operations on |ψ⟩. They are never the same thing.
