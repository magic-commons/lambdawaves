MIR logo — where these two files come from, and what may be done with them

mir-light.svg   1 979 bytes   for LIGHT grounds   copied byte for byte from Josh's Library logos, JL-LOGOS/light/mir-light.svg
mir-dark.svg    1 978 bytes   for DARK grounds    copied byte for byte from Josh's Library logos, JL-LOGOS/dark/mir-dark.svg
Copied 2026-10-01.  They are Joshua Hosain's approved logos; do not redraw them.  Replace them only with a newer copy
from the same folder.

What they are (the folder's README): the MIR wordmark in Butler Free Extra Bold, OUTLINED (the letters are paths, no
font is embedded or needed), with the large nine-tile rainbow diamond.  Use the light file on light backgrounds and
the dark file on dark backgrounds.  mir/shell/gui.js shows the one that matches the theme.

The typeface the letters were outlined from:
  Butler (free version) — © Fabian De Smet, www.fabiandesmet.com.  "Butler" is a Reserved Font Name.
  Its EULA (v2.00, in JL-LOGOS/licenses/Butler-EULA-v2-00.pdf) permits use "for personal and commercial creative
  work" and the publishing of original designs made with it.  These files are such a design: the kit does not
  redistribute the typeface itself, so the font file and its EULA are not shipped here.  The ABOUT page of the GUI
  window credits the typeface and links the designer's site.
