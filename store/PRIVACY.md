# λWAVES privacy policy

Draft of 2026-09-24. It takes effect when λWAVES is first listed in a store. The web version of this text is `store/privacy.html`, which is published at `https://lambdawaves.magic-commons.com/privacy.html`. Keep the two files identical in content.

**Short version: λWAVES collects no data about you. Nothing you do in it is sent to us or to anyone else.**

## Who this covers

λWAVES is made by Joshua Hosain (Magic Commons). This policy covers every edition of λWAVES:

- the **web** edition at lambdawaves.magic-commons.com;
- the **desktop** edition sold on **Steam** (Windows and Linux);
- the **iPad** edition on the **App Store**;
- the **Android** edition on **Google Play**.

## What λWAVES collects

Nothing. λWAVES has:

- no accounts and no sign-in;
- no analytics, telemetry or crash reporting;
- no advertising and no tracking;
- no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. It does not use Steamworks, Game Center, Google Play services or Firebase.

## What stays on your device

- **Projects, notes, settings and window layouts.** These are kept in the app's local storage on your device. We cannot see them. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, they are deleted.
- **Files you export**, such as project files, PNG pictures and video recordings. They are saved where you choose and nowhere else.
- **Share links.** COPY LINK puts the state of the instrument into a link on your clipboard. It is sent only if you paste it somewhere yourself.

## The microphone

The microphone is used only when you turn on audio input by adding an AUDIO device to the modulation rack. Your device asks for permission first, and you can refuse. λWAVES analyses the sound in memory, on your device, to drive the modulation. It never records, stores or sends the sound. The microphone is released when you remove the last AUDIO device. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your device's or browser's settings.

## Keeping and deleting data

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to keep, hand over or delete. What λWAVES stores on your device stays until you delete it: remove a project in PROJECTS, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.

## Network use

- The **desktop and iPad** editions contain the whole app and make no network requests of their own.
- The **web and Android** editions load the app's files from lambdawaves.magic-commons.com, which is served by Cloudflare. Like any web server, Cloudflare keeps operational logs of those requests, such as IP address, time and browser type, under Cloudflare's own privacy policy. We add no analytics or tracking to the site, and we do not use those logs to identify anyone. The Android edition runs inside Google Chrome (as a Trusted Web Activity), so Chrome's own settings and policies also apply.
- **Links in the app**, such as the credits and "return home", open in your web browser only when you tap or click them.

## The stores

Steam (Valve), the App Store (Apple) and Google Play (Google) handle purchases, downloads and updates under their own privacy policies. λWAVES does not receive personal data about you from them through the app.

## Children

λWAVES is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone of any age.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new date. A change that would start collecting data would also need a new app version and new store privacy answers first.
