# Google Play graphics: λWAVES

## What Play requires (read 2026-09-24, https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151)

| Asset | Rule | This kit |
|---|---|---|
| App icon | 512 × 512, "32-bit PNG (with alpha)", ≤ 1024 KB | `icon-512-32bit.png`: the app's own `lab/img/icon-512.png` (Gemini mark), re-saved as RGBA, still opaque, 13.6 KB |
| Feature graphic | 1024 × 500, "JPEG or 24-bit PNG (no alpha)" | **DRAFT** `feature-graphic-1024x500-DRAFT.png`, RGB, made by `store/capsules.py` from a real field frame plus the λ + WAVES wordmark |
| Screenshots | JPEG or 24-bit PNG with no alpha; each side 320–3840 px, and the long side at most twice the short side; up to 8 per device type; at least 2 across device types to publish | see below |
| Phone screenshots, to be eligible for promotion | "at least four screenshots with minimum 1080px resolution": 16:9 landscape (≥ 1920 × 1080) or 9:16 portrait (≥ 1080 × 1920) | **needs Josh's phone** |
| Large screens (tablets, Chromebooks) | at least 4, 1,080–7,680 px, 16:9 or 9:16 | optional; the Steam set fits (below) |
| Preview video | a YouTube URL, public or unlisted, monetisation off, not age-restricted | optional; the Steam trailer draft could be uploaded unlisted once its audio is cleared |
| Text in graphics | "Taglines should not take up more than 20% of the image"; avoid "Best", "#1", "Top", "New", "Discount", "Sale" | the drafts carry only the name |
