## The shape (the law of this cut)
**THE FIRST NON-SPATIAL SYSTEM.** A two-level register — ONE qubit |ψ⟩ = a|↑⟩ + b|↓⟩ — lives beside the 91-label register in its own window, BLOCH, of the
LADDER's kind (own register, own canvas, no field write): the Bloch vector (⟨σ_x⟩, ⟨σ_y⟩, ⟨σ_z⟩) drawn on ORBIT's sphere painter (the same canvas code, one sphere),
its trail over the last period, the Hamiltonian H = (ω₀/2)σ_z + (Ω/2)(cos φ(t) σ_x + sin φ(t) σ_y) with the drive's phase φ(t) = ∫ω(t)dt — LARMOR (Ω = 0: the
closed form precession), RABI (ω = ω₀ on resonance: P↓(t) = sin²(Ωt/2) exactly in the rotating frame; off resonance the generalised Rabi formula), LANDAU–ZENER
(a chirp ω(t) = ω₀ + βt through resonance: the asymptotic flip probability P = 1 − e^{−πΩ²/(2β)} as the PREDICTION, the stepper's measured P beside it) — stepped
exactly by the 2 × 2 unitary per dt (a closed-form exponential of a 2 × 2 Hermitian matrix, never a series), the dt halved and re-solved to prove the step (the
plan's gate: the two runs agree to 10⁻⁶ or the readout says the step is too coarse). THE BELL PAIR is TWO qubits in the same window as a second mode: |Φ⁺⟩ =
(|↑↑⟩ + |↓↓⟩)/√2 and its three siblings, the reduced density matrix of one qubit and its von Neumann entropy S = ln 2 exactly (a product state 0), drawn as two
Bloch vectors of length 1 − 2·(mixedness) — a Bell pair's two vectors have length 0 and the window says why. MASS SCALING is one knob on SPECTRUM, μ/mₑ
(1 · positronium ½ · muonic hydrogen 185.84 — the reduced masses), rescaling r → r/μ and E → μE exactly as `setZ` rescales for Z (`hamiltonian.js:185`; RADIATION
already uses the reduced mass): hydrogen's picture, rates and periods follow; the kernel's tables are the same tables under a scale. THE LATTICE PRESETS are three
rows in `lattice-model.js`' table: HOFSTADTER (a square L × L under a flux Φ per plaquette — the butterfly is a record for later; here the spectrum at one Φ with
the Harper equation's E(k) printed at Φ = p/q small), QUANTUM WALK (the Hadamard walk on the chain as a two-component site register: the coin is the second
component; variance ∝ t² printed against the measured) and SPIN CHAIN (the XX chain of N ≤ 8 spins as a lattice of 2^N ≤ 256 sites by Jordan–Wigner for the XX
case — exact; the half-chain entanglement entropy after a quench from |↑↓↑↓…⟩ printed, its Page-law bound said).
