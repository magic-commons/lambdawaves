## WHAT STANDS (read before judging the rows)
- THE PROJECT IS THE ARRANGEMENT: a `.lambdawaves.json` carries the register, the look, the camera, the modulation and the kit's timeline doc (`presentation.timeline`: lanes, curves in beats with NORMALISED values, clips), and `__LW.projects.importText/open/save/exportText` are its doors (RESEARCH §1–2). The NODES graph is a device record, not a project key (nodes.js:97).
- EVERY KNOB IS A TARGET and every surface action is an `action.<id>` trigger (RESEARCH §3); the registry's `toNorm(id, value)` turns a value in the knob's own unit into a curve's `v`. What is missing is ABSOLUTE style/view/palette: a score cannot say "bar 7 is GLASS on PHOSPHOR".
- THE AUDIO CLIP is a lookup (envelope at 100 Hz inside the project), one band per clip, KEEP bounces the file; 025 proved the bounce frame- and sample-exact and showed the screen (NODES, overlays, labels) on the film (RESEARCH §4–5).
- THE BOUNCE IS SCRIPTABLE: `tools/perf/film-obey.mjs` sets the card's fields and presses RENDER from node through the gate's Firefox; the film is a blob a script can read (RESEARCH §5).
- A FILM IS A SCREEN RECORDING NO MORE (025) — and a SCORE IS A SCREEN SESSION NO MORE (026): a Claude does not drag clips; it writes the arrangement.

## THE LAW OF 026 (to docs/laws/film.md at the close)
THE CONDUCTOR: a film is authored as a SCORE — one JSON in bars, beats and the targets' own units (`docs/conductor/SCORE.md`) — conducted by `lab/conduct.js` through the app's live doors into a project (register, look, camera, nodes, lanes, audio clips, triggers) and bounced from node by `tools/conduct.mjs` (the kit's recorder, the card's own fields, the audit read back), which leaves the MP4, the project file, the audit and a contact sheet beside each other. THE SCORE THINKS IN BARS: every time in a score is a beat on the ruler at the score's tempo; a cool effect lands on a divisor of the bar (¼ · ½ · 1 · 2 · 4 bars), never on a second. A Claude reviews a film by its contact sheet and re-conducts; the project a score leaves is a project like any other (FOLDERS, DEMOS). THE ICON SAYS WHAT IT IS: MOLECULES wears a hex ring, NODES its three nodes.
