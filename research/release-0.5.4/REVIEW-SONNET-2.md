Three of its facts do not survive the tree: **36 of 38 geometries say UNVERIFIED, not 26** (and C₆₀ is a DRAFT geometry); **water and the live-only molecules have no catalogue row, so P3's "no oracle → UNCHECKED" prints UNCHECKED on the best-checked molecule in the repo**; and 0.5.3's INFORMATIONAL window is **closed by default on first run**, so for a first-time reader (a phone folds it too) THE HEAD is the only seat that ever shows.
The one thing I would still change: make the STATUS string ONE pure, DOM-free, node-importable builder (the window, THE HEAD, the caption, hydrogen and `tools/export-record.mjs` all call it; its numbers are read from the constants the tests assert) before any BRIEF — today `chem.capability()` is a chemview closure that hydrogen, node and the head cannot share.
