## The shape (the law of this cut)
**THE RADIAL BREATH.** For the register ψ = Σ c_nlm R_nl(r) Y_lm, the radial probability density P(r, t) = r² Σ_{l,m} |Σ_n c_nlm(t) R_nl(r)|² is a CLOSED FORM
diagonal in (l, m) (Y_lm orthonormal), the coefficients evolving as e^{−iE_n t} — so P(r, t) is the one-dimensional truth of a packet going out and coming back,
drawn as a small line plot in CALCULUS's new BREATH tile over r ∈ [0, half] at 256 points, redrawn at CALCULUS's cadence (its `may()` gate), with the time
cursor on the period. Its two integrals are its oracles, both printed: ∫P dr = Σ|c|² (the norm, to 10⁻¹⁰ by the quadrature) and ∫rP dr = ⟨r⟩ — which CALCULUS already
computes by a different road (the bilinear form in the coefficients), so the two numbers agree or the tile says by how much they do not. The REVIVAL tap makes
the Rydberg packet the shipped `rydberg` preset is (circular n = 4…6) and sets RATE so that LADDER's classical period T_cl = 2πn̄³ shows as one second — the
breath you can see — with T_rev = (n̄/3)·T_cl printed from LADDER's own law (the two windows name one number). THE A(t) TRACE is |⟨ψ(0)|ψ(t)⟩|² — `state.js`'
`autocorrelation(t)` — drawn over ONE REPEATS period (the transport's period from `period.js`, or the horizon when none exists) as a line in a second CALCULUS
tile, with the current t as a cursor, the revivals as the peaks, a hover naming t and A: the beats the equalizer shapes, seen in time. THE UNCERTAINTY ELLIPSOID
is the covariance Σ_ij = ⟨r_i r_j⟩ − ⟨r_i⟩⟨r_j⟩ of the position, its three principal axes √λ_k drawn as an ellipsoid wireframe about the bead (0.8.2 P3's
expectation point, the same overlay canvas), from the matrix elements ⟨a|r_i r_j|b⟩ — the radial integral ∫R_a R_b r⁴ dr (one more power than the dipole's) and
the angular Gaunt integrals of Y_lm Y_1m′ Y_1m″ products reduced to l = 0 and l = 2 — built once per register version; the trace Σ_ii = ⟨r²⟩ − |⟨r⟩|² is its
oracle (CALCULUS computes ⟨r²⟩ by its own road for hydrogen: ⟨r²⟩_nl = n²(5n² + 1 − 3l(l + 1))/2 for a single label — printed exact beside the measured for a
one-label state), the 1s ellipsoid a sphere of radius 1 (⟨r²⟩ = 3, ⟨r⟩ = 1.5 → σ² = 3 − 2.25 = 0.75 per … — the builder computes and prints the exact number
and the test holds it). Everything lives in CALCULUS (two tiles) and the overlay (the ellipsoid); nothing touches the field.
