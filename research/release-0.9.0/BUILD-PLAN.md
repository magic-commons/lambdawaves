the ABOUT face in the notebook glass, `docs/`, the MODEL sheet's prose) is reworded sentence by sentence against the tree: every accuracy claim becomes the

("no beat above 3 a second on screen unless you turn the guard off" — there is no off: say what the law does); the ChronusQ credit becomes a reference with its
licence line (GPL-2 vs GPL-3.0-only: cited, never merged); a PySCF table (the 38 records + the live molecules' oracle deltas, generated from `library.json` and
the oracle fixtures by a `tools/` step) joins README. **THE PROVENANCE REGISTRY** is `docs/PROVENANCE.md` GENERATED by `tools/provenance.mjs` from the catalogue
and the fixtures: every record's source geometry (its `source` sentence), licence (the basis sets' BSE licence, the records' own), oracle (which PySCF, which
script, which fixture, the delta), the script that made it, the cut it arrived in — one table, `--check` byte-exact like the catalogue. Sol's A9 archive (the C++

prints, and the fixture's provenance. **THE FIELD LAW ON A DEVICE** becomes a standing browser test (hydrogen 1s + 2p_z vs the closed form; water vs the f64

its fingerprint; the iPad's row is filled by hand when Josh runs it (the test prints a line he can paste). **SOL'S CERTIFICATES** as gated readouts: where a
