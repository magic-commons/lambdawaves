## ROUND 1 · FABLE (lead) — 2026-10-07

### 0 · The reading, in one paragraph

"Cyclotomic scaling" in the corpus's sense is the character-basis principle: a kernel that commutes with a group is diagonal in the group's characters, and for a cyclic group Z/n the characters are the n-th roots of unity — the DFT, with values in Q(ζ_n). In the molecular engine the group is the molecule's point group G acting on the AO space V (dim n = 36 for benzene); the "kernels" are S, F (one-electron) and the ERI tensor (ij|kl) (two-electron); the "scaling" is the fraction of each that survives. Three distinct numbers wear the one word "fraction", and this round names them: **(a)** the non-zero fraction of the tensor in a symmetry-adapted basis (what a blocked Fock build or RPA touches), **(b)** the number of *independent* integrals (what any exact-symmetry code must compute and store, basis-independent), and **(c)** the petite list — the orbits of G on `md.js`'s own loop units, the shell quartets (what the live loop would skip). They differ, and for a planar molecule they differ by a factor two in a way the 1/|G| folklore hides (Prop. 4).

### 2 · The derivation

**Prop. 1 (the selection rule; KNOWN — Wigner–Eckart, Wigner 1927 / Eckart 1930; Schur).** The Coulomb kernel commutes with every isometry of the framework, so the ERI tensor is a G-invariant element of (V*)⊗⁴; in a basis adapted to irreps with partner labels, (ij|kl) ≠ 0 only if the trivial irrep occurs in Γ_i ⊗ Γ_j ⊗ Γ_k ⊗ Γ_l. For one-dimensional real irreps this is Γ_iΓ_j = Γ_kΓ_l; for a cyclic group with complex characters ζ^k it is the grading −k_p + k_q − k_r + k_s ≡ 0 (mod n) (the conjugate on the bra indices of the chemist's integral). MEASURED exact in every group above (0 violations). For two-dimensional irreps the label rule is only necessary: D₆h's label rule allows 0.2683 of the tensor, the partner basis has 0.1352 non-zero, and the independent count is 0.0525 — the difference is Clebsch–Gordan structure (Prop. 3).

**Prop. 2 (the Abelian count is the fourth moment of the AO character; DERIVED-HERE, MEASURED exact).** Let G be Abelian with grades k_μ on the adapted basis and block sizes n_k. The number of non-zero entries of the full tensor is N₄ = Σ_λ a_λ² with a_λ = #{(i,j) : k_j − k_i = λ} = Σ_k n_k n_{k+λ}, the autocorrelation of the block-size function on Ĝ. Its Fourier transform is the AO character, n̂(R) = Σ_k n_k χ_k(R) = χ_V(R), so by Parseval

  **N₄ = (1/h) Σ_R |χ_V(R)|⁴,  i.e.  f = N₄/n⁴ = 1/h + (1/h) Σ_{R≠E} (χ_V(R)/n)⁴ ≥ 1/h,**

with equality iff χ_V(R) = 0 for every R ≠ E — iff G acts freely on the AO basis (no atom fixed by any non-identity operation). Likewise Σ_k n_k² = (1/h) Σ_R |χ_V(R)|² (the Fock blocks). Benzene: D₂h, χ = (36, 0, 0, 4, 0, 24, 8, 0): (36⁴ + 4⁴ + 24⁴ + 8⁴)/8 = 2,015,744/8 = **251,968**, the measured count to the integral; Σn² = (36² + 4² + 24² + 8²)/8 = 244 ✓. The mirror alone, (24/36)⁴/8 = 0.02469, is 98.8 % of the 0.02500 excess over 1/8 — **the planar σ_h, not the C₂ axes, is why benzene's D₂h fraction is 0.150 and not 0.125**. C₆ acts freely (χ = 0 off E): f = 1/6 exactly ✓. C₆h: (36⁴ + 24⁴)/12 = 167,616 ✓. The cyclotomic reading's cleanest sentence: *the ring character gives exactly 1/n because a ring acts freely; every mirror and every axis through an atom raises the fraction by its character's fourth power.* (The same identity gives N₄ = dim (V⊗⁴)^G for any group — for D₆h, 84,352 — but for a non-Abelian group that is the count of invariants, not of non-zero entries in a partner basis: 227,008 measured.)

**Prop. 3 (the independent count; KNOWN formula — the inner product of characters, Serre *Linear Representations of Finite Groups* §2.3; DERIVED-HERE as applied).** With the 8-fold permutation symmetry the ERI tensor lives in Sym²(Sym²V*); the number of independent integrals under G is

  **dim (Sym²Sym²V)^G = (1/h) Σ_R χ_{S²S²V}(R),  χ_{S²W}(R) = (χ_W(R)² + χ_W(R²))/2.**

For G = {E} it is N(N+1)/2 with N = n(n+1)/2: 222,111 for n = 36 (406 for n = 7). For an Abelian group with real 1-D irreps it equals the number of label-allowed canonical quartets, and for a signed-permutation representation it equals the Burnside orbit count minus the orbits with a sign stabiliser — the three measured 34,187s (and 154s) are this one theorem seen from three sides. For D₆h it is 11,666 (5.25 %, against 1/24 = 4.17 %); the partner-adapted basis carries 30,755 non-zero canonical entries, **2.64× the independent count** — the redundancy a Wigner–Eckart reduced-matrix-element storage removes and a plain SAO basis does not. **This is the sense in which "~1/|G|" is true: as a count of independent numbers, basis-free, approached as the group's action on the AOs becomes free; never as the non-zero fraction of a non-Abelian partner basis.**

**Prop. 4 (planar molecules pay the mirror as zeros, not as orbits; DERIVED-HERE, MEASURED).** If σ_h fixes every atom, M(σ_h) is diagonal (±1) on the AO basis, so σ_h fixes every shell quartet as a set and every AO quartet up to sign. Burnside: #orbits = (1/h) Σ_R Fix(R) ≥ (Fix(E) + Fix(σ_h))/h = 2N/h. **The petite list of a planar molecule saves at most h/2 on the loop's units**, and σ_h's share is paid as forced zeros — the quartets with an odd number of σ_h-odd functions. Benzene D₂h: shell orbits 11,678 ≥ 2·45,150/8 = 11,287.5 (the largest orbit has 4 members, never 8); AO forced zeros 88,408 = 39.8 %. D₆h: 4,116 ≥ 2·45,150/24 = 3,762.5. Water: σ(yz) fixes all three atoms, 162/406 = 39.9 % forced zeros. **Corollary for `md.js`:** today's loop computes those 39.8 % and gets 8.6e-18; a component-level skip inside `quartetAcc` (the (ia, jc) loops, l.371–384) would drop them, but the primitive R-tables (`rInto`, once per primitive quartet, l.363) shrink only with the shell-level petite list — two cost centres, two fractions (25.9 % and 15.4 %).

**Prop. 5 (the ring: Bloch, Hückel and the crystallographic restriction; KNOWN — Bloch 1929, Hückel 1931; DERIVED-HERE as stated; MEASURED).** For n equivalent sites with b functions each and C_n acting freely, the complex SAOs are the Bloch sums φ_k = n^{−1/2} Σ_m ζ_n^{km} χ_m; S and F restricted to any symmetry-equivalent AO set are circulant, so their generalised eigenproblem is solved by the DFT: ε_k = F̃(k)/S̃(k), F̃(k) = Σ_m F_{0m} ζ^{km}. For benzene's six p_z this is the live Fock's π spectrum to 1.9e-14 with no eigensolver. **Integrality:** ζ₆ + ζ₆⁻¹ = 1, so 2cos(2πk/6) ∈ {2, 1, −1, −2} and the four π energies are *rational* functions of eight matrix elements:
  ε(a2u) = (F₀₀ + 2F₀₁ + 2F₀₂ + F₀₃)/(S₀₀ + 2S₀₁ + 2S₀₂ + S₀₃), ε(e1g) = (F₀₀ + F₀₁ − F₀₂ − F₀₃)/(…), ε(e2u) = (F₀₀ − F₀₁ − F₀₂ + F₀₃)/(…), ε(b2g) = (F₀₀ − 2F₀₁ + 2F₀₂ − F₀₃)/(…).
The DFT matrix has entries in Z[ζ₆]/√6; its real subfield is Q. This is the **crystallographic restriction** (KNOWN: tr R = 1 + 2cos θ ∈ Z ⇔ n ∈ {1, 2, 3, 4, 6}): the C_n adaptation is exact over Q for exactly those n, over Q(√5) for n = 5 and 10 (the cyclopentadienyl rings of ferrocene; the S₁₀ axis of C₆₀), over Q(√2) for n = 8, over a degree-φ(n)/2 field in general. What "cyclotomic" adds beyond symmetry adaptation is therefore **only about the adaptation, never about the integrals**: the AO integrals are Boys-function values at the ring's distance pattern (transcendental); the SAO integrals are their Z[ζ_n]/n-linear combinations, exact in the character field — and §39's "association kernels diagonalise in character bases" is, for the ring, Hückel's theorem: the circulant algebra of Z/6 is the Bose–Mesner algebra of the cyclic association scheme, F_π and S_π are elements of it, and their common eigenbasis is the character basis. The same statement for L-0408: det of the π Fock block = Π_k F̃(k), a product of "periods" of the Fock circulant — a norm-like quantity in the character field.

**Prop. 6 (the grid; DERIVED-HERE, MEASURED).** With n even and cell-centred sampling (field.js l.189–190) the voxel centres sit at half-integers in voxel units and none lies on a coordinate plane or axis, so every sign-flip operation acts freely and the asymmetric unit under the signed-permutation subgroup G ∩ O_h is exactly n³/|G ∩ O_h| with **zero boundary** (D₂h: 1/8; C₂v: 1/4; T_d molecules built on cube diagonals as `td()` does: 1/24; an O_h molecule: 1/48).
