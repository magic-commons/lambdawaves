## THE LAWS OF 022 (to CLAUDE.md at the close)
- ONE MENU DOOR: every context menu is the kit's `contextMenu({ x, y, entries })` (submenus, ✓/•, icons, the Escape stack); a hold-tap IS a right-click (`TAP.hold` 500 ms, `TAP.slop` 8 px, `-webkit-touch-callout: none` on every surface that has a menu); a surface with a menu never shows the browser's.
- A POPUP WEARS THE SKIN: every popup list is `.glass[data-mir-surface="menu"]` and takes its material only from the skin's tokens (`--surface-fill/-veil/-sheen/-edge/-radius/-shadow-menu`, `--state-hover/-on/-on-rim`, `--surface-menu-floor`); its rows lay out as they like; no native `<select>` anywhere.
- RELIEF IS TIGHT: a raised control's highlight reaches 1–2 px (never 6), the shine ≤ 2 px at α ≤ .35, the light lift capped; nothing in lab/ reads `--neu-raise` raw — `--relief-raise` (and `-well`, `-press`) always, so RELIEF FLAT reaches everything.
- A HINT IS ONE LINE: ≤ 60 characters through `hint()`; the second sentence lives in the window note or ⓘ; a glyph replaces the words where one exists; an action's bar hint is its short `hint`, not its label; a label is never wider than its control.
- A ROW FITS: a control row is `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(--cell-min, 1fr))`, the knob face scales to its cell between `--knob-min` and `--knob-max`, switches and segs stretch, and a row never adds height for a control that fits; CURVES, the sphere, pictures and the CSB are the exceptions.
- THE SURFACE IS A SURFACE: the CSB expanded is TRANSPORT · MACROS (a named, valued, paged knob bank) · FAVOURITES (slots) · METERS, on one well with section heads; nothing scrolls inside the docked card.
- AVAILABILITY IS ONE LAW: `availability(control) → { on, why }` decides greyed vs hidden vs shown per mode and system; a greyed control keeps its hint and says why.
