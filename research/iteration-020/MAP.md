## EXPERIMENTAL — the play stutter and the "buffer" (read-only diagnosis; file:line at 7e5d6db)
THE LOOP: `lab/clock.js:30–39` `advance(nowSec)`: `dw = nowSec − _wall` clamped to [0, 0.1], `t += rate·dw` — WALL-CLOCK steps; `play()` anchors `_wall` (`:51–55`); nothing writes `t` per frame during plain play (scrub/reset/step are user/preset/restore/film only). `lab/rack.js` `loop(nowMs)`: `:344` occlusion refresh if dirty or every 300 ms (a layout read); `:362–397` the modulation tick; `:399–402` `dt = clock.advance(now)`; `:425–426` PACE hold (`field.paced && field.inFlight > 3` → `tier = NONE` AFTER the clock advanced); `:429–432` FIELD CLOCK cap (`fieldRate.capMs`, 0 by default); `:434–440` the present `field.frame(...)`; `:443–466` AUTO SCALE / governor then the pause edge; `:593–596` re-arm. `modesAt(t)` = `reg.at(t)` closed form (`state.js:211`). `field.frame` (`field.js:1982–2002`): one encoder, one submit — compute into `psiTex` (N³ rgba16float) then the full-screen march; no await, no readback on the frame path; `onSubmittedWorkDone` only counts in-flight when `paced` (`:2000`; Firefox is never paced, `:2462–2466`). THE CAUSES, ranked: (1) wall-clock Δt with uneven presents — a late vsync is a double step, a held frame (Chromium) advances t and shows nothing, Firefox's uncapped queue stamps t at rAF time and shows it later; a recorder sharing the GPU makes it worse (017 P: the field's frame is the dominant GPU cost). (2) the ray-march jitter re-seeded EVERY present, only while playing: `field.js:1761` `v[19] = (stats.presents % 97) / 97`, read at `:909` (hash `:541`, ESP `:901`) — a boil that starts at PLAY and that encoders hate; the film pins it (`render-exact.js:686` `JITTER_SEED = 13`). (3) AUTO SCALE canvas snaps in the first second of play (`rack.js:443,454–459`; scale can step UP when `median ≤ 1.08·budget`, `controller.js:46–48`; a possible limit cycle above 120 Hz, inferred). (4) temporal aliasing at high RATE (`flash-law.js:5–8`, warned on the RATE knob). (5) only under a molecule: VIBRATE's 32-pose ring (`chemview.js:1299`), a FIELD CLOCK cap, the worker-paced RT run (`:834–847`). (6) the 300 ms occlusion layout read (017 P: ≈ 4 ms/s).
