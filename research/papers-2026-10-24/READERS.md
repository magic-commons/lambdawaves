## Paper 4 — KNOTTED ZEROS IN SEVEN LEVELS
- **Sir Michael Berry** (H. H. Wills Physics Laboratory, University of Bristol). Author of *Knotted zeros in the quantum states of hydrogen*, Found. Phys.
  31, 659 (2001) — the paper rebuilt, corrected (his eq. 4) and lowered here. Known to answer correspondence. FIRST reader for Paper 4, after the novelty
  search (NOVELTY-CHECK-PAPER-4-2026-10-25.md).
- **Mark Dennis** (University of Birmingham). Knotted optical vortices and knotted polynomial fields (Dennis, King, Jack, O'Holleran & Padgett 2010; Bode &
  Dennis). The paper's Prop. 2 (polynomial knots × e^{−r/n} are not hydrogen states) speaks to his construction.
- **Daniel Peralta-Salas** and **Alberto Enciso** (ICMAT, Madrid). *Dislocations of arbitrary topology in Coulomb eigenfunctions* (2018): the existence
  theorems for knotted Coulomb nodal lines; the paper's n_G(p, q) bound is the quantitative question beside their qualitative one.

## Paper 3 — THE LEAK AND THE REPAIR
- **Paolo Lazzeretti** (Università di Modena e Reggio Emilia). Decades on current-density conservation in molecules (magnetically induced currents, the
  continuity defect in finite bases). FIRST reader for Paper 3.
- **Ward Struyve** (KU Leuven). Quantum trajectories, the current's definition and its conservation.
- **Ángel S. Sanz** (Universidad Complutense de Madrid). Bohmian/quantum trajectories in molecular systems; would read the repair's wall identity critically.
