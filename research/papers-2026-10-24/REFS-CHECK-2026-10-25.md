# References check: the six Draft 08 additions (25 October 2026)

Six references were added in Draft 08 and marked "(Added in Draft 08 …)". Each one was checked against its Crossref record (api.crossref.org, `query.bibliographic`, then the work record by DOI). Each Crossref record was deposited by the publisher: Springer, The Royal Society, AIP Publishing, Elsevier, ACS and APS. All six matched, so no reference is UNVERIFIED.

**What changed in the manuscripts.** Each of the six bullets gained one DOI link in the house format that the other references already use: `[doi:X](https://doi.org/X)`. The link sits right after the bibliographic data, before the "(Added in Draft 08 …)" note, which matches where the other references carry their DOI. In the Bialynicki-Birula bullet the DOI goes before the existing arXiv link, separated by `; `, which is the house order (compare Robinett and Zaman Ahmed). No other line was touched. A script checked that exactly three lines changed in each file: 01 lines 300–302, 02 lines 338–340.

The "(Added in Draft 08; to be confirmed)" notes were left as they were, because the task allowed only the DOI to be added. They can now be shortened to "(Added in Draft 08.)".
