## The mathematics (B.4, sonnet2-f — KNOWN/DERIVED/MEASURED)
Pitch map: f = ΔE·RATE/2π with RATE in a.u. per wall second (3000 → 72.6 fs/s; 1s–2p 0.375 Eh → 179.049 Hz; the top beat 1–6 232.1 Hz; the six levels 238.7,
59.7, 26.5, 14.9, 9.5, 6.6 Hz unfolded). The Strang step is S-unitary to ≈ 2.8·10⁻¹⁵ a step (`opus1-e`; 91 labels, 2.0 ms per 100 steps at dt 0.5); the drive at
E₀ = 10⁻² departs from the RWA by 8.8·10⁻³ with dt 0.5 and 0.1 agreeing — the honest check is a dt-halving re-solve, which the builder runs once by hand on the
179 Hz tone, not as a gate. The hydrogen z matrix couples l ↔ l ± 1 at equal m only; its radial integral for 1s–2p₀ is 128√2/243. Folding: f′ = f·2^k with k the
integer putting f′ in [220, 440); the three notes from E_n ∝ 1/n² are exact octave relations {1,2,4}, {3,6}, {5}. Over-barrier field of level n: F_c ≈ 1/(16 n⁴) a.u.
(n = 3: 7.7·10⁻⁴ a.u. = 0.40 GV/m; 1 a.u. = 5.142·10¹¹ V/m).
