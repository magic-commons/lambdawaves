## 1. Summary (12 lines)

1. λWAVES is the ancestor of the family: the modulation host controller, the rack shell, the keyboard editor, the 3-D camera card, the palette editor and the notebook all began here and were copied outward. The harvest flows back only where a sibling grew something new.
2. The cheapest large win is **provenance**: CITE (EARTH), honesty chips (EARTH's CANONICAL / DERIVED / ARTISTIC, AUTOMATA's per-law contract, SOLEIL's DERIVED), and a figure that re-opens as the project (AUTOMATA PRINT). λWAVES already writes every window's STATUS in code; none of it is on screen as a chip, and nothing cites its sources.
3. The biggest structural fit: **λWAVES is closed-form in time**, ψ(t) = Σ c e^(-iEt) at any t with no integration. Everything AUTOMATA needed checkpoints, replays and hash-takes for (scrub, loop, fork, frame-exact export) is free here, so the kit's TIMELINE and RENDER suit λWAVES better than any sibling, and a loop can be exactly one physical period (`period.js`).
4. Best unclaimed combination: **SCENES/MORPH + named orbitals + fly-to** (AUTOMATA P1/PF1 + EARTH E8a): "go to 3d_z²" is an eased cross-fade of the register plus a camera move to a derived best view, from one search box.
5. Biggest gaps against the siblings: no layers/strata, no scenes, no library gallery, no parameter-plane map, no event marks, no figure provenance, no post chain, no title card.
6. In the MIR 1.5 kit already (alpha.23): XY (PAIR/ROUTE/MORPH), LANES, RAMP, CURVES, CAMERA, GRADE, TIMELINE, PATTERN, RENDER, FOLDERS, HISTORY, INFORMATIONAL pages with leader lines, opener (title card), flash guard, adaptive ink, PNG-carries-project, SESSION/RESUME, probe, LLM skill.
7. Not in the kit (so kit-candidates, since two or more apps wrote them): SCENES + FADE, parameter-plane SHEET/MAP (WEDGE), places/INDEX search, CITE, honesty chips, MIDI notes/CLOCK/LEARN, events-fire-triggers, units/scale bar, grid-layout (periodic-table) gallery, event marks on the ruler, an FX chain.
8. **Hard dependency:** λWAVES is frozen on MIR 1.4.3 by Josh's word. Every "kit: yes" row needs the 1.5 adoption first. AUTOMATA's dry run measured that adoption at 3 of 375 laws red, two copied CSS rules to re-scope, and −3,627 lines of copied shell deleted; λWAVES carries more app-owned shell (`rack.js` 5,566 lines, `modwindow.js`), so its dividend is larger and its risk is its own (the three state scopes, the byte-exact `new-project` file, the digest lock).
9. Twelve rows score fit 5. Most need no engine change: the field and the maths stay as they are.
10. The anti-harvest is mostly about **honesty**: parameter fields, kaleidoscope and symmetry folds, warp/CA/grain, Monte-Carlo splat banks and recorded-frame banks would each break an exactness law or draw a symmetry the state does not have.
11. Three things none of the apps has that λWAVES could give the kit (§5): a closed-form clock with period-snapping loops, live-bound math labels, and a known-answer lamp.
12. Order that follows the dependencies (a suggestion, not a plan): app-only first (CITE, chips, names/search, scenes, scale bar, events), then the 1.5 adoption and the kit rows that ride on it (FIGURE, EXPORT, TIMELINE, INFORMATIONAL, FOLDERS, title card, ink, flash guard), then the big builds (MAPS, LAYERS, ATLAS).

## 5. THREE IDEAS NONE OF THE APPS HAS, THAT λWAVES AS FLAGSHIP COULD GIVE THE KIT

1. **A closed-form clock: random-access time with a declared period.** The kit's timeline assumes time is something an app *plays through* (BASINS needs a recorder to freeze it, AUTOMATA needs a checkpoint ring to scrub, SOLEIL needs banks). λWAVES is the one app whose state at time t is a function. Let an app declare `at(t)` (random access) and `period() → T | null`, and the kit gives it: scrub with no cost, **loop brackets that snap to the exact period**, reverse for free, RENDER of one seamless period, SYNC = PERIOD for the modulation clock, and a ruler that marks the period and its harmonics. EARTH's `setExternalClock` is the nearest cousin; this is the general contract, and the rest of the family (AUTOMATA's Q2R is exactly reversible; SOLEIL's rotations are periodic; EARTH's days) could declare a period too.
2. **Live-bound math labels.** INFORMATIONAL pages are static markdown + KaTeX. λWAVES already turns the live state into LaTeX in Josh's notebook spelling (`latex-state.js`, `copyLatex`). Let a page carry placeholders (`{{state}}`, `{{E}}`, `{{T}}`, `{{ΔE}}`) that the app answers from `describe()` and the layer re-typesets when the value changes (throttled, paused when idle, honest when the value is not exact), with the leader line following the feature. Then "the 3d_z² orbital" and its energy, node count and current phase are always the ones on screen. No sibling has a page that updates itself, and it fits Josh's own INFORMATIONAL brainstorm (maths in LaTeX that is cursor aware).
3. **A known-answer lamp: verifiable honesty in the UI.** The family's laws are "every claim has a measurement", but the measurement lives in `tests/` and the reader never sees it. Pair each honesty chip (row 4) with a **VERIFY** action that runs the window's known-answer check live in a worker (norm to 3e-9, the Lyman-α period, GPU voxels against the CPU to 2e-5, the benzene bright pair at 0.3903) and lights a lamp with the number and the build. The kit supplies the chip, the lamp and the worker port; each app registers `{status, oracle}`. AUTOMATA's ENGINE "contract" and EARTH's CANONICAL / DERIVED / ARTISTIC are labels; this makes the label checkable by the person reading it.
