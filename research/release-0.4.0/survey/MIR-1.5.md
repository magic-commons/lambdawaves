## 3. THE INFORMATIONAL

`mir/info/` (layer 789 lines, page 160, bodies 171, seats 100, leader 101, `info.css`, `faces.css`); doc `docs/INFORMATIONAL.md`; gallery `gallery/info.html`; plan in the vault `MIR CLAUDE 1.5.X INFORMATIONAL + LLM PLAN 2026-10-01` (all eight rulings "Yes to all with defaults", 10-01).

**What is built.**
- **Pages are plain `.md`** (the same pages the notebook shows as tabs; Obsidian-readable). `---` on its own line starts a new BLOCK (blocks stand in one column beside the subject). Fences/CRLF/BOM/front matter handled. Maths via the notebook's sanitised marked + KaTeX (loaded lazily from `shell/vendor`).
- **Callouts are labels**: `> [!mir|anchor word?] Title` then `>` body lines. Anchor kinds: **feature** (`nucleus`, a name the app resolves through `features()`), **place** (`@0.39+0.68i`, `@51.48,-0.00`; the app's own coordinates through `place(text) -> {x,y,r}|null` in stage px), **control** (`ui:zoom-knob` = the element with `data-info~="zoom-knob"`, else `id=`). Optional line word: `flat`/`flat-first`/`diagonal`/`diagonal-first`. Other callouts (`note`, `tip`) stay prose. No tutorial, tour, slide, or title-card machinery (Josh: "that's what the multiple pages are for").
- **The greeting** is only a position: page 0, shown when the project opens if it has text and `pages.shouldGreet()`; `greet(...,{first:true})` shows only up to its first `---`; leaves on Escape or the first stage press after a 2.5 s hold. Notebook: project pages are tabs (YOURS first, then the project's; page 0 marked 0; eye = `shared` to a visiting model); the shelf (`mir.notes`) is the personal store; COPY TO SHELF / PROJECT copies, never links.
- **Bare vs pane**: bare = ink over a feathered radial shade (`::before`) + `text-shadow` halo, no box; pane (`addBlock({pane:true})`, `setPane`) = the same type on the kit's pane tokens (`--surface-fill/-edge/-radius/-shadow`; TINTED never blurs, REFRACTIVE carries `--surface-filter` = a `backdrop-filter`).
- **Seats** (`seats.js`): a scored chooser decides which side of its anchor a label (or a comb) rests on; a crossing costs 100,000, pushed-back 1000/px, overlap 3/area, "not the natural seat" +800, "the seat it has" -2500; sticky until trouble (score without preferences < 6000); a changed seat holds 1.4 s. A block rests on the far side of the subject from the pointer (changes side after the pointer crossed the middle by 8 % and stayed 350 ms); with no room it sits over the subject dimmed (`data-over`); `avoid()` rects (rack columns, transport bar) are never covered by blocks.
- **Lines** (`leader.js`): only flat, 45° and vertical, legal at every frame. **diagonal-first** names a feature (thing -> 45° -> flat shelf under the title; default for a titled label on a named feature). **flat-first** is a note (thing -> flat -> 45° -> text; default for a place, a control, or no title). Several labels on one anchor share a comb (one 45° run, a spine, a flat branch each). A line never crosses words (16 px clearance vertically). A control's line lives on a second root, `.mir-info-over` (fixed, above the chrome, `pointer-events:none`), because controls sit in windows that paint over the stage.
- **Springs / parallax / drift** (numbers: `docs/INFORMATIONAL.md` "The numbers"; constants `INFO` at `layer.js:54`): label spring k 70 s^-2, zeta 0.6 (about 9 % overshoot, ~0.8 s); block spring k 34, zeta 0.72; neighbours rest 18 px apart, bump only < 2 px; pointer reach 260 px, 5200 px/s^2 at contact, heat tau 0.9 s ("reaching is not fleeing": on the block, within 110 px, or heading at it within 60° never moves it and lifts it to full strength); walls 10 px; parallax lean block 38 px / label 22-30 px, tau 0.28 s; **drift** (a bob, 5 / 3.5 px, periods 8-14 s) off by default; hold-still = the `I` key (a held key row `info-hold`, 480 ms touch press); a pin shows once. Entrance ~0.7 s (dot 140, line 60+260, text 200 + 30/line, stagger 55 ms); exit ~0.3 s; reduced motion = a 120 ms opacity fade.
- **Edit**: `setEdit(true)` lets labels be dragged (the rest follows the hand; Escape rolls back).

**What runs per frame (the GPU-recording cost).**
- At rest: **nothing** (no rAF, zero reads/writes/frames; tested over 600 ms, also with a page and a live `ui:` label).
- While anything moves (a spring settling ~0.8 s, a drag, parallax easing tau 0.28 s, a **camera move that calls `viewChanged()`**): one `frame.write(paint)` per frame (`layer.js:373`): `translate` set per label/block (`setVar`), `data-*` attributes, and `d`/`cx`/`cy` on the shared full-stage SVG (halo + ink path per group + a dot). `viewChanged()` additionally runs `frame.read` (measure) + `computeRests` + `bodies.js` rest resolution (12 passes) + the seat chooser; for a continuously orbiting WebGPU camera this is continuous main-thread work plus a full-stage SVG repaint.
- Observers: one `ResizeObserver` on the stage; one `MutationObserver` on `<body>` **only while a `ui:` label exists** (style/class/hidden/open outside the layer; ignores its own writes); controls re-read on transitionend/scroll.
- Drift on: a frame booked forever (off by default; on in the gallery page).
- Compositor: bare mode = text-shadow halo + one radial-gradient `::before` per label (paint, no backdrop); pane mode = a `backdrop-filter` per block over the WebGPU canvas (the kit measured "the rack's blurred glass halves the pan frame rate over a moving picture: ~30 fps with the rack shown, 60 hidden" on BASINS before and after adoption; nothing was changed). No cost meter for the layer exists; the GUI cost line counts blur/shadow/shine elements.
- **Not proven by the kit**: touch (long press, avoiding fingers), WebKit/iPad, and "how it looks over a WebGPU canvas" (headless screenshots of that canvas are black; ink-over-picture modes AUTO/INVERT/THRESHOLD were planned, AUTO first; **INVERT and THRESHOLD are not built**).

## 4. THE XY PAD and THE CAMERA ROW

**The pad** (`mir/controls/xy.js:127 xyPad`, sheet `controls/controls.css:19,138`; docs CONTROLS.md, PANEL-XY.md):
```js
xyPad({ label, aria, x: {min,max,value,log,step,fmt,unit,...}, y: {...}, home: [x,y], tags: {bl,br,tl,tr}, onInput(x,y), onChange(x,y), cls })
  -> { root, pad, x, y, get() -> [x,y], set(x,y), setDisabled(on), paint(), lattice(reset), destroy() }   // x, y are the kit's knob() widgets
```
- **Contract** (AUTOMATA's MORPH law): one pad for the HAND + two real knobs that stay the modulation targets; a macro routes onto a knob, never the pad. The pad paints FROM the knobs (the modulated reading = accent-B dot; the hand's base = a ring), so it cannot disagree with them. `control({pair:[axisX,axisY]})` builds it from a descriptor.
- **Look**: no well (since alpha.21: "the lattice is the tint"). A square grid of dots on a transparent canvas under the dot and the ring (`createLattice`, **private**, `xy.js:47`): each dot swells toward the point as a smooth bell and its ink rises from the well's tint (`--xy-lattice-ink` = `var(--glass-well)`, alpha included) to the accent (accent B when routed); a gentler swell sits under the mouse and eases in/out (`--motion-micro`) and steps aside while dragging. Eight tokens on `.mir-xy`, all `skin:true`: `--xy-lattice-n` (auto = side / pitch), `-pitch` 10px, `-dot` .9px (far radius), `-swell` 3.1px, `-reach` .35 (bell radius as a share of the side), `-hover` .5, `-far` 1 (share of the ink's alpha), `-near` .9, plus `-ink`. **"A little less intense, smaller circles for λWAVES" = lower `-swell`/`-near`, `-dot` in λWAVES' own sheet; no kit change.** Cost: ~0.45 ms a paint at 240 px / 2x while dragging, 0.25 ms hovering; repaints only when the point, cursor, size or theme changes (one `frame.write`); nothing at rest. `lattice(reset)` is the test probe (`tests/xy-lattice.browser.mjs`, 22 rows).
- **Touch/hand rules**: `touch-action:none`; pointer capture; a press brings the dot to the pointer unless the fine gear is on; **any modifier or a second finger = the 1/8 fine gear** (a virtual point, nothing jumps when it engages); double-tap = home (320 ms today; wave 22 call 20 makes it **300 ms within 14 px, everywhere**); arrows nudge 0.01 (Shift = fine, Page = x10), Home/Delete/Backspace = home; the cursor swell never runs on touch, never under reduced/no motion (`fxAllowed`); `--xy-size` 240 px max (the kit's 44 px touch law applies to the two knobs beside it); pad wheel does not scroll the rack (`stopPropagation`). The input mode (touch vs precision) is `core/kbm.js`, decided by the live pointer event.
- **XY panel** (`panels/xy.js:93`): one pad, three uses on a segment: **PAIR** (the app's coupled pairs: BASINS pan, POLAR yaw/pitch...), **ROUTE** (two macros `xy:x`/`xy:y` owned by the panel via `mod.own`, the modulation window's own grips), **MORPH** (AUTOMATA's four-corner snapshot blend over any window's dials; SPRING switch returns to centre on release; a 12-point trail while routed). Needs `installModulation({roots:['xy',...]})`.

**The camera row** (`mir/panels/camera.js:402`, `camera-rig.js:73 VOCAB`, `docs/PANEL-CAMERA.md`): a rack card from a PORT `{get(id), set(id,v), subscribe(fn), has?, angle:'deg'|'rad', ranges?, modIds?, verbs?, home?, north?, northMemo?, turn?(dyaw,dpitch)}`. Rows (each only if the port has the ids): verbs (the app's own first, then PAN HOME + NORTH in 2-D or HOME in 3-D) / mode TURNTABLE-FREE / direction **sphere** (yaw, pitch; the tip follows the finger; damped least squares, no pole lock) / **PAN pad + X, Y knobs with ROTATION under Y** (alpha.20) / view (ROLL, ZOOM, FOV, FLIP) / scales (ROT SCALE, PAN SCALE, ORBIT, ORBIT ANGLE) / HAND (DRAG, FRICTION, INERTIA, FLING, AUTO-ROTATE, SPIN, WHEEL smooth|step). Modulation targets are `camera.<id lowercased>`; a hand on a routed dial writes its base. Idle: it subscribes, no poll (BASINS' 250 ms re-read is gone).

**How BASINS drives its 2-D camera with it** (`app/camera-port.js`, 58 lines): `get`/`set` over the view itself (`gestures.js viewRotation/setViewRotation/viewPan/setViewPan`) or the registry for `rotationMul panMul orbit orbitAngle`; `subscribe` = the engine's `onViewChange` from the loop's per-frame compass update; `north: northToggle` + `northMemo`; one verb `CENTER` (a promise disables the button until it settles, a refusal is a toast); `project:false` (camera rides BASINS' look part), no panel history (BASINS' own CAMERA domain); pad X/Y are `panX/panY` = shares of the view's width, up = +Y; knobs are bound to registry ids with `mod.bindKnob`. It deletes `camera-window.js` (157) and `camera-rig.js` (26).

**Could the kit drive λWAVES' 3-D camera (orbit + pan)?** Mostly yes; what is missing:
- *Already there*: the kit panel was designed from λWAVES' own window ("TURNTABLE · FREE, SPIN, FRICTION, DRAG GAIN, FLING, ZOOM, FOV"). Port: `mode` (turntable/free = `obs.mode`), `yaw`, `pitch`, `roll` (FREE), `zoom`, `fov`, `autoRotate`, `spin`, `friction`, `drag` (DRAG GAIN), `fling`, `turn: orbitBy` (the one road for a relative turn), `home: resetView`, `angle:'rad'`, `ranges` for λWAVES' units (distance 1.2-8, FOV 0.25-1.2 rad, SPIN 0.02-2 rad/s, FRICTION 0-12). `docs/PANEL-CAMERA.md` says λWAVES deletes ~37 lines (`rack.js` 1521-1557) and keeps `camera-law.js` + a port. Per the scope table the camera FEEL is PREFERENCE and the pose/mode PROJECT but not history: pass `project:false`, no `history`.
- *Missing in the kit*: (1) **a 3-D pan / lens shift**. The pan pad is built whenever the port has `panX` and `panY` (`camera.js:305`, not gated on mode), but the 3-D `HOME` ignores pan (`homeIds` for 3-D = yaw, pitch, roll, zoom, fov, `camera.js:258`), `createCssPort` 3-D (`transform3d`) has no pan, and "pan" semantics (a principal-point offset so a max-FOV, far-zoom view sits off-centre like a planet photo) is engine work in `lab/field.js` ray generation (a screen-space offset uniform). (2) The **camera law** (ambient + residual `omega(t) = omega_amb + d e^{-mu t}`, closed-form fling travel, FREE quaternion with levelling slerp, eased key orbit) is `lab/camera-law.js` (161) + `stage-gestures.js` (121) and is nowhere in the kit; the panel only has dials. (3) Camera key rows (WASD eased orbit, Shift+Q/E POV, Ctrl+Shift+Q/E dolly zoom) are held `up` rows the app writes. (4) Yaw wraps but the pad is clamped 0..1 (the sphere is the kit's answer for yaw/pitch; the pad for a flat pair). (5) `zoom` in the vocabulary is "nearness" (log .25-64, home 1); λWAVES stores a distance -> port-side inverse mapping.
- *A pad as an orbit JOYSTICK* (position = angular velocity, SPRING on) would suit λWAVES' law (the residual relaxes to the ambient drive) but is not a kit feature; the XY panel's SPRING switch is the closest.
