## The shape (the law of this cut)
UHF is RHF twice with one Coulomb: Fσ = h + J(Dα + Dβ) − K(Dσ), σ ∈ {α, β}; the SCF loop of `lab/scf.js` `rhf` runs with two densities, one DIIS over the
concatenated error vectors, the same Löwdin frame, the same round-off floor; nα = (N + 2S)/2, nβ = (N − 2S)/2 from the entry's `multiplicity` 2S+1. The
card's D is Dα + Dβ (Tr(DS) = N exactly as today — every consumer of `sol.D` is untouched); the SPIN density is Dα − Dβ, drawn by the field's existing
`'signed'` kind (the n² matrix without the square root — the Δρ road, READ as 'real': two-colour lobes). ⟨S²⟩ = S(S+1) + nβ − Σᵢⱼ|⟨φᵢᵅ|φⱼᵝ⟩|² is printed,
never hidden: the contamination IS the picture's honesty. The closed-shell singlet (multiplicity 1) keeps the RHF road byte for byte — `uhf` is only entered
for multiplicity > 1 (or by the H₂ CURVE's own ask). PySCF UHF is the oracle (10⁻⁸ Eh and ⟨S²⟩ to 10⁻⁶), never a tuned gate. No ROHF, no CASSCF, no
open-shell TDA/RT in this cut: the RT run, TDA, the drive and the register STAND DOWN with a sentence on an open-shell solve (their closed-shell machinery
would be a lie there).
