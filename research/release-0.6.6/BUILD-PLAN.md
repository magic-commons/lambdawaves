## The shape (my 15:45 note, now the law of this cut)
Gemma never touches the DOM. She emits a short JSON list of VERBS from a WHITELIST; the app runs them through its own hand roads (`loadPreset(…, { hand: true })`,
the MOLECULE pick, the camera port, the layout verbs, the notebook's own text road), and ONE ask is ONE history row ("GEMMA · benzene"), so undo takes a whole turn
back. Every number in her prose comes from the app's INVENTORY (the molecule head's stem numbers, the STATUS line, the populated states' prose, REPEATS, the camera
pose) — the molecule head's law ("every number the record's stem") applied to a chat model. She runs OUTSIDE the browser: a local Gemma 4 behind a small HTTPS
broker on the workstation (the userspace Ollama at `~/ollama` from the Odysseus setup; the stage owns the GPU, and an in-page model would break the stutter law and
the iPad's memory). The PWA stays weight-free; nothing leaves the LAN. Gemma 4 is on Ollama since 2026-04 as `gemma4:e2b · e4b · 12b · 26b · 31b`; on the RTX 3070
(8 GB, the stage using 1–4 GB) the default is **`gemma4:e4b`** (≈ 4.5–5.5 GB at Q4); `gemma4:12b` (≈ 6.7 GB) is an option when the stage is light. Ollama's
structured outputs (`format: <JSON schema>`) constrain her grammar, which is what makes a 4B-class model reliable at emitting verbs.

close any window; pose the camera, auto-rotate, home; set RATE within the flash law, play, pause, scrub; turn LOCATE / PARTICLES / STAGE FORMULA / ATOM LABELS on;
pick an equalizer preset; write a paragraph on the stage and a block into the notebook with the app's numbers. Cannot (this cut): edit the register's amplitudes,
route modulation, save or export, change the look, read the picture (she has no eyes — the inventory is text; a screenshot road is a later cut), or claim a number
the inventory does not carry.
