## The mathematics (D.6, RU — KNOWN/DERIVED/MEASURED)
Φ(r) = Σ_A Z_A/\|r−R_A\| − Σ_pq D_pq ⟨p\|1/\|r−r′\|\|q⟩; the second term is the one-electron nuclear-attraction integral with the centre at r and Z = 1 (Boys
functions through `rtable`/`boysInto`, already in `md.js`). Free-space Poisson on a zero-padded grid: Φ_el = G ⊛ ρ with G = 1/r on the doubled box (the Hockney–Eastwood
trick: no periodic images); the self-cell value G(0) = 2.38·h (the cube's mean 1/r), the plan's "analytic core split" is the nuclei kept analytic. The grid's error
is the sampling of ρ's cusps at nuclei — hence ≤ 3 % on water's lone-pair line (the plan), ≤ 10⁻³ a.u. on benzene's smoother ring. The card's lazy pass costs one
trilinear sample per texel at `res`; the frame thread does nothing. The card never claims "real-time" (the paper's law).
