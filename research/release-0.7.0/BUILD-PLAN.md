## The shape (the law of this cut)
A molecule's normal modes are a RECORD: computed once by a `tools/` step (PySCF's analytic RHF Hessian at the record's own geometry as the oracle, OUR
RHF's curvature along each mode as the check), stored beside the orbital records, fetched on the first VIBRATE press. The picture is PURE in Q(t): the
nuclei move as R(t) = R₀ + A·Q₀·cos(ω_k t)·L_k, and the density is **LINEAR + ONE McWEENY STEP** — D(Q) = McWeeny(D₀ + Q·D₁ₖ, S(Q)) with D₁ₖ the record's
dD/dQ in the MOVING AO basis (gauge-free; D, never C) and S(Q) the exact overlap at the displaced geometry — built in the CHEM worker as a table of K phase
samples once per (mode, amplitude), uploaded by the frame thread with no maths on it (the RUN pump's shape). The caption says what it is: "STO-3G RHF
harmonic, as computed". No geometry is optimised (PySCF has no optimiser in `sci`, and the stage's density IS the record's geometry): the Hessian is
taken there with translations and rotations projected out, which is what "as computed" means. Q₀ ≡ √(ħ/(μₖωₖ)) is the classical turning point of the
zero-point energy ½ħω (a.u.; μₖ = Σ mᵢ|Lₖ,ᵢ|² for |Lₖ| = 1), the AMPLITUDE dial is in units of Q₀ (0 … 4, default 1), and past 2 Q₀ the label says
"not harmonic here". THE FLASH LAW holds here as it holds for RATE: a breath that would show above 3 Hz on screen (`lab/flash-law.js` `aliasOf([0, ωₖ],
RATE, fps)` > `FLASH_HZ`) is HELD at Q = 0 and the row says so in one sentence; BREATHE sets RATE so one breath takes a second, so it always plays.
