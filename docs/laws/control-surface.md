## The laws of 034

THE BAR'S HOME IS THE BOTTOM (034 F3, Josh 11-02: "CSB seems stuck to the top … I would prefer it towards the bottom"; supersedes 032 T13's memory): `modDodge` seats the bar `narrow ? top : free(bottom) ? bottom : free(top) ? top : where it is` — on top only while a window holds the bottom (TIMELINE + MODULATION docked below), back at the bottom the moment the bottom frees. A dodge's landing is never a choice: `lw.csb.side` is RETIRED (removed at boot); a hand's own choice of side, if one ever comes (a drag), would be its own record.
