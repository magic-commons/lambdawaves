## The law of 036

INFORMATIONAL IS THE PROJECT'S (036 S4, Josh 2026-11-03: "Also other things to make sure that reset is modulation window, timeline's settings/content, informational and molecules/atomic settings"). Every row of `INFO_ROWS` — the words, the labels, the seat, the ESP bar, the announcements, the cursor, BALL AND STICK, the bonds, the marks, STATUS — rides in the project as `presentation.info`, only the rows off their default (`shell/stage-words.js` `infoOfProject`, default-absent: NEW, the demos and every older file carry none); an open puts them in place through `loadInfo` (a row the file does not name goes to its default), so NEW returns INFORMATIONAL to the first run (the formula and the atom labels ON, the rest OFF); an UNDO leaves it (never a history row); a RESUME of a session written before 036 keeps the record's. ## The laws of 037 — WIDGETS

A WIDGET IS A STAGE OBJECT, NOT A WINDOW (037 W, Josh 11-05: "a new class of windows on the stage, extremely similar to 'Informational's architecture … users can add as many copies of the same widgets as they want"). THE LAYER: `lab/widgets.js` `createWidgets(app)` makes `#widgets` in `#lab` beside the kit's info layer at z 19 — over the atom labels (9), under INFORMATIONAL's words (20) and every window (30) — in the words' ink (`lab.css`), never a pointer target outside EDIT, out under H (`body.ui-hidden`) and under the menu; a widget is `section.lw-widget[data-kind][data-id]` with no background, border or shadow ("empty CSS"), its box `{ x, y, w, h }` in STAGE FRACTIONS (a layout survives a resize). THE RECORD: `lambdawaves.q0.widgets` `{ items: [{ id, kind, x, y, w, h, opts }] }` (RECORDS, PROJECT) is the live one a reload boots from; the project carries it as `presentation.widgets` in BOTH scopes (`shell/persist.js` `widgetsOfProject` · `loadWidgets`), default-absent — NEW, the demos and every file before 037 open with none, a session written before it keeps the record's — so A PROJECT RESETS THE STAGE (Josh's 036 answer 2) and every hand's change is ONE named row of the undo ring: WIDGET ADD · WIDGET REMOVE · WIDGETS CLEAR · WIDGET MOVE · WIDGET SIZE · WIDGET STYLE · WIDGET TEXT (· SIZE). An item of a kind not registered is kept in the record and drawn when its kind registers. THE TICK: `tickWidgets(nowMs)` is the rack's one call in the CPU frame (`lab/rack.js`, after the stage text); the layer stands down on a hidden page and under H, never because a window is closed; a forced tick (a mount, a resize, the theme, an opts change, EDIT) runs in its own animation frame when the rack is idle. `__LW.widgets` is the layer.

EDIT AND SNAP (037 W2): under EDIT the widgets — not the layer, so the empty stage still turns the camera — take the pointer: a thin dashed outline (the accent when one has the keys), the kind's word above it, × at its top-right, the grip at its bottom-right, its kind's controls under it; a drag moves, the grip sizes (≥ 0.06 of the stage each way, held inside it); SNAP (on, this session's — no key) holds the moving edges (a move's left · centre · right and top · middle · bottom, a size's right and bottom) on the nearest line within 6 px among every other widget's edges and centres and the stage's edges and centre lines, a 1-px accent guide across the stage while it holds, gone on release; the record is written on release; arrows nudge the focused widget 1 % (Shift 5 %); Escape leaves EDIT through the escape stack (`lab/actions.js`).

THE MANAGER (037 W3): the rack card `widgets` (`lab/widgets-window.js`, glyph `widgets`, KIND core, CLOSED on a first run, in the FINDER and WINDOW, `window.widgets.*` generated) IS the widgets' library: the gallery of the registered kinds (glyph over word; a press adds one at the stage's centre, each next one stepped 2 %), the placed list (word · its number among its kind · ×; its own scroller past six rows, FOLDERS' manner, never the window's), EDIT · SNAP · CLEAR.
