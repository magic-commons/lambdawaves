# The main menu, the Picker and the library

The paragraphs below were moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md at 021 D2 and keep their iteration tags; where an older line names a file, a seat or an id a later iteration retired, the later law wins, and the laws of 021 stand first. THE MAIN MENU (034) stands over them.

## The laws of 044

THE HUE RING'S FIRST CLICK ARMS (044 R5, Josh 11-13; the hand's rule of 11-12, "it has to click then the scroll is available"): in the library, a plain click on the hue ring that does not yet hold the focus only arms it — it takes the focus, so the wheel turns it (the kit's `wheelTurn`, 10° a notch) — and a click on an armed ring flips colour ↔ mono as before; a drag turns it armed or not, a double-click puts the hue at 0, a key's press (Enter, Space) flips at once (`lab/picker.js`: the focus is read in the capture phase, before the kit's press takes it). The LOOK button keeps its bare wheel (Josh: "They can interact with scroll").

THE DESKTOP BUILD SHOWS NO AI MARKS (044 S1, Josh 11-12: "steam version does not show logo"): under the desktop host (`globalThis.lwavesDesktop`, the preload's one word) `lab/picker.js` builds no credits strip on the face and `lab/loader.js` asks for neither of its two faces; `tools/platform/electron/package.mjs` removes `img/ai/` and `img/menu/ai/` from the package, so the marks and the unlicensed OpenAI face are not in a Steam folder at all. The web build is as it was. ABOUT's sentence naming the agents stays on every host.
TWO VOLUMES ON THE SHELF (044 S3, Josh 11-12: "Just release Volume 1 and 2 for now. Vol 3 will be a post release expansion. (Only show 2 volumes for now)"): `lab/volumes.js` `HELD = ['vol-3']` is the one place that says which volumes are not offered yet, on every host. `shelf().volumes` (EXPANSIONS' shelf, the volume stepper, MOLECULES' pages, the search) leaves a held volume out unless this device already holds some of it (its cache `lw-vol-<id>` exists); `shelf().all` and every by-id road (`index`, `file`, `installMolecule`, a project's or a link's molecule) still read its entry, which stays in `volumes.json` un-renumbered. Releasing a volume is deleting its id from HELD. The desktop's packaging and what is not built: docs/release/STEAM.md §8.
THE DESKTOP FACE HAS NO WORDMARK AND NO TIP JAR (044 S5 · S7, Josh 11-12: "you can remove it outright", "Remove some steam version"): under `globalThis.lwavesDesktop` the face builds no MIR wordmark (bottom right; no seat moves because it is gone), EXPANSIONS' shelf builds no ko-fi card and an install asks for no tip. The web keeps all three.
A VOLUME THE DESKTOP SERVES IS INSTALLED (044 S8, Josh 11-12: "As free add-ons"): the desktop host answers `volumes/volumes.json` from the volume folders its package holds (each with its own `volume.json`; `tools/platform/electron/main.cjs` `shelf()`), so there a shelf entry with an `index` is on the disk — `lab/volumes.js` `served(v)`. `installStatus` says installed (`served: true`), `kept` is every molecule, `install` · `installMolecule` · `installFile` are ok and copy nothing, `remove` refuses, `file(…, { network: false })` reads the package, and no sheet is kept as DATA. The card reads INSTALLED with nothing to press; an empty desktop shelf says the volumes are free add-ons on Steam. docs/release/STEAM.md §8.
A SHEET IS A PICTURE (044 S9): `tools/build-deploy.mjs` marks a volume `drawing` (the card's WIP) only for molecules with neither a `thumb` nor a cell in `t/pages.json`'s `density` sheet.
