## THE LOADING SCREEN (036 G7, Josh 11-03, FOR JOSH 035 answer 2: "a loading screen that goes from 0% to 100% with the MIR logo filling up like a defragging kind of heuristic … everything for the whole app loads with the fonts, videos, built in molecules... everything except the expansions")

## THE COLLAGE (iteration 035, Josh 11-03; 036 THE COLLAGE'S EDITS; the vault's `research/iteration-035/` and `iteration-036/` `C-BUILD.md`)

"The main menu background could be cycling square videos … each row, alternating, slides past each other … after 12 seconds, during a moment where the grid is perfectly tiled, the motion rule could switch to 'Alt. columns'." The face lies over a wall of square λWAVES clips; the decks, FOLDERS and the tutorial keep the plain ground.
- ONE VIDEO, MANY TILES: never one `<video>` a tile. Each theme has ONE ATLAS, `lab/img/menu/collage/<theme>.mp4` — 12 × 7 = 84 SLOTS of 160 px (1920 × 1120), 24 s = 12 BEATS of 2 s, **30 fps** (036: the sources are 60 and 30, and 24 made a 3/2 judder of the one and dropped every fifth frame of the other), H.264, a keyframe every 8 s — its poster `<theme>.webp` (the first frame, 960 × 560) and `collage.json` (`tile, cols, rows, fps, dur, beat`, and per theme `slots: [{ off, clips: [12 family indexes] }]`, `families: [{ src, look, lum }]` — the source, its look group, its windows' mean luma). Slot k sits at column k mod 12, row ⌊k / 12⌋; it plays `clips[j]` over [off + 2j, off + 2j + 2) modulo 24, off = 0.5 s × (k mod 4), so the cuts never land together and the loop point runs on inside clip 11 (the strip opens with its tail, closes with its head). `lab/picker-collage.js` `createCollage(root, { theme, still, lite })` puts `canvas.mm-collage` and `div.mm-veil` first in `#lwMenu`; the picker calls `show()` in `showView('face')`, `hide()` for any other view and in `close()`; the kit's `onThemeChange` swaps the atlas. One `<video>` (not in the DOM, its native `loop`: the wrap costs no frame) plays; each NEW VIDEO FRAME (`requestVideoFrameCallback`, else the frame count) is copied once to a buffer and every tile is a blit from it at 30 Hz (at P/6 px/s a step is under a pixel).
- THE PAUSE (036 C4, Josh: "the Collage itself sometimes freezes for a split second every now and then"): a stall of 100–230 ms about every 10 s on Josh's machine is Firefox's hardware WebRender road (NVIDIA): a bare page with one small canvas redrawn 30 times a second shows it, software WebRender does not, and no app callback runs long at it — nothing in lab/ causes it. Strips slid by compositor animations were tried and dropped: Firefox composites canvases redrawn inside animated layers at half the rate (the vault's iteration-036 C-BUILD.md).
- THE DRAW: tile pitch P = clamp(150, 11vw, 190) px, an even count of device px, NO EDGE — no gap, the tiles abut (GAP 0, 036 C3, Josh: "every video must touch each other with no noticeable outline"); the canvas at min(devicePixelRatio, 1.5), every tile a whole device px, the slot inset 2 px (x264's cross-slot bleed), and the canvas as large as the turned view (the tilt a CSS rotate, a random 1.5–3.5° either way, chosen once a load). ROWS: even rows drift +P/6 px/s, odd rows −P/6, wrapping; the lattice is tiled each time the lines have slid half a tile apart (every 3 s), and at the first tiled instant 12 s or more after the last turn (every 12 s) the wall turns to COLUMNS (and back) with nothing moving at that instant. A tile is a window onto a slot. NO TWINS: a slot is never chosen while it plays within 3 tiles (Chebyshev) of the tile; among the rest a tile takes the slot least seen on screen whose family is none of its neighbours' (the family rule gives way first), so a 1600 × 1000 view shows a twin only when the slots run short, and never nearer than 3 tiles. At its slot's cut a tile jumps half the time (always when the sliding lines have brought its family beside it or its slot within 3 tiles), and on every wrap (off screen), to a slot a second or more from its next cut by the same rules. The wall runs only while the face is shown and the page visible; a resize (debounced) keeps the mode, the leg's clock and the lattice's phase; a dip of the video holds its last frame (the poster only before the first frame and in stillness); `collage.json` and the poster are kept on the device (library.js `keepData`). THE GROUND AT THE FACE is plain, the wall or not (under FROST the blur would show the rack's chips through the corners); the decks and the tutorial keep the frost. THE VIGNETTE (036 C3, Josh: "reduce the fade effect into just a vignette"): the veil is a radial of the ground from 0 in the middle to .5 at the rim, no flat tint, no bands; the face logo wears a drop shadow in both themes (in LIGHT under its halo of the ground); CONTINUE wears a soft plate of the ground (.6, 6-px radius) at .8.
- STILLNESS: `still()` (reduced motion, MOTION reduced or off) and PERFORMANCE LIGHT play no video: the poster drawn once through the same deal, the tilt kept. The wall looks again once a second while it is shown, so a MOTION or PERFORMANCE change under the open menu holds it on the poster (or plays it again) within a second.
- THE MEASURE (036 C1, Josh: "inspect some of the videos for stutter and lag … don't confuse them for the incredibly fast rate motion videos"): `menu-collage-scan.py --rescore` reads every candidate window at its source's native rate (96-px grey centre square, each frame's mean |Δ| from the last); a frame is new when its |Δ| passes the window's own floor (a held frame of a recording carries a little x264 residue, never exactly 0); speed is never stutter, only holds are: STUTTERY (036 C1b, calibrated on the eye's 36 filmstrips) = it moves (its 75th-percentile |Δ| ≥ 4: a slow drift or a near-still scene never is) and somewhere in it a hold of cadence + 3 frames ends in a jump (over 2.5 × the window's median |Δ|). A stuttery window tries four other starts inside its segment; the cut refuses what stays stuttery. Filmstrips (12 native frames in a row) go to `.tmp/collage/strips/` for the eye.
- THE DEAL (`tools/menu-collage.mjs atlas`): slot k's twelve clips come round-robin over the families (a family is one source video; a family leaves the round once its clips have each been dealt twice the pool's mean), never one clip twice in a slot, never one family twice running (the wrap included), at no instant more than 4 slots on one family, and no LOOK GROUP (the source's folder and, for a dated recording, the name before the date, case folded — one session's recordings and its edit are one look) over 25 % of a theme's plays; a seeded PRNG, so a rebuild is the same atlas. THE CAPS (036 C2, never relaxed, spread evenly over the twelve beats): the look 'black molecules' (the folder and BLACK MOLECULES.mp4) ≤ 8 % of a theme's plays (Josh: "in Light mode, don't use too many of the 'Black Molecules'"); in LIGHT a dark-ground window (lum < 100) ≤ 12 %; THE VANILLA RULE — a recording dated 2026-10-27 or later (NODES landed that day; the name's date, else the file's) ≤ 10 % in both themes (Josh: "I would prefer the non-node recordings … to be more close to the vanilla"); no readers' films.
- TO REBUILD (the tool's head names them): `python3 tools/menu-collage-scan.py ~/Videos` (THE SCOUT: clean 2.4-s windows between cuts, never a still, centre-square thumbs and contact sheets → `.tmp/collage/`) → THE EYE (a cheap model writes `.tmp/collage/verdicts.jsonl`, a line a candidate `{ id, material, ui, info, theme, grade, note }`; a second look appends a line, the last line for an id wins) → `python3 tools/menu-collage-scan.py --rescore` (THE MEASURE) → `node tools/menu-collage.mjs cut` (THE POOL: λWAVES, grade ≥ 2, no UI, no informational — an atom's letters on its balls are its picture — never a stuttery window; a theme under 48 clips admits info 1, then ui 1, only up to 48, and says so; 30 fps by frame count: a 60-fps source keeps every second frame) → `node tools/menu-collage.mjs atlas` (30 fps, veryslow, CRF 31, 32 while a file passes 14.5 MB) → `node tests/pwa.test.mjs --write`. The pool stays in `.tmp/`; the atlases, posters and `collage.json` are committed. `img/menu/` is DATA: never precached.
