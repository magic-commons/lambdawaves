## THE MAIN MENU (iteration 034, Josh 11-02; the vault's `research/iteration-034/PLAN.md` chain M)

"It's time to finally make it a 'Main Menu'." The Picker is the MAIN MENU in every word a reader sees (the `menu` action's label, WINDOW › TOOLS › MAIN MENU, the tile rows MAIN MENU › …); the file stays `lab/picker.js`, the ids `menu` / `picker.*`, the hooks `#lwMenu` and `mm-*`, the facade `app.state.picker`.
- THE LANDING FACE (`.mm-face`, picker.js `showFace`): the λWAVES logo large over two doors, TUTORIAL · LIBRARY (album art in THE CARD'S LIGHT, the λWAVES palette, the word in LW Title), and ENTER THE LAB, quiet, under them. It is the home: every open lands there — the boot after the notice, `?menu=1`, L, WINDOW › MAIN MENU — and the head's ⌂ comes back to it; Escape and ENTER THE LAB leave for the lab. The face lies OVER the decks, which stay laid out under it, hidden and inert, so LIBRARY is instant. Its entrance: the logo drops, the doors rise in turn, then the way to the lab (none under reduced motion).
- LIBRARY opens the decks on MOLECULAR, ATOMIC above it and EXPANSIONS below it (the decks' first showing is MOLECULAR; after that they stay where the reader left them). A first run's LIBRARY deals MOLECULAR's first page in THE ENTRANCE (012 L3), which now follows the face rather than the notice. The CSB's LIBRARY seat (the mode column's, `windows/molecules.js`) still reads LIBRARY and opens the decks directly (`toggle({ decks: true })`); `api.show(id)` opens the decks on that section.
- EXPANSIONS is the third deck's word (it read LIBRARY: THE VOLUMES, their shelf, THIS DEVICE); its id, its icon and its CSS hooks stay `library`.
- ONE VIEW AT A TIME (`showView`): the face, the decks, FOLDERS' gallery (015 M4) or the tutorial — the last two in the decks' seat. Leaving a view puts its hero to rest.
- THE TUTORIAL DECK (`.mm-tut`, `showTutorial`): a scrollable grid of 9:16 cards, as many across as fit off the racks (five at 1600 px) and two at a narrow width, one per row of `TUTORIAL` (picker.js: `{ id, title, caption, themes }`) in its order — each the volume card's album art (its still in the theme in use, its hero, THE CARD'S LIGHT), its number and title at the art's foot, its caption under it ("caption to come" until Josh's words). The hero loops while a fine mouse is on it or a key's focus (never under reduced motion); a press plays or pauses it (a touch's way). No hover hint: the words are in its aria name only.
- ONE HERO PLAYS AT A TIME in the whole menu (`artOf`: a play rests the last), volumes and tutorial alike.
- THE HEROES' TOOL: `node tools/tutorial-heroes.mjs <tut-id> <clip> [--theme dark|light] [--title …] [--caption …] [--x 0…1]` makes `lab/img/menu/tut/<id>.<theme>.mp4` (720 × 1280, 30 fps, H.264, no sound, faststart — the volume heroes' recipe) and its still from the mp4's first frame (540 × 960), and writes the row. A landscape clip is COVER-CROPPED to its 9:16 strip at `--x` (the middle 32 % of a 16:9 recording: the field, not the racks), so a portrait recording is the honest source; the theme is the recording's (measured on its whole first frame unless `--theme`), and one clip serves both themes until the other's comes. `--caption <id> "…"` sets the words, `--drop <id>` takes a card away; then `pwa --write`. The seed (034) is all 25 clips of `~/Documents/LAMBDAWAVES/LANDING PAGE VIDEOS/` in the PLAN's order (HERO 1–6, IMPULSE, LFO, MACROS, MOLECULES, NOTEBOOK, PALETTE, PROJECTS, SPECTRUM, THEMES AND CUSTOMS, WAVE, WAVE 2, AUDIO, ENV, MOD HERO 1–6), titles from the file names; Josh's recordings replace them one by one. `img/menu/` is DATA: never precached.
- THE FLAG ALONE: with the racks hidden (`body.rack-hidden`) or the interface out (H, `body.ui-hidden`) the title is the MIR flag alone, 22 px, in its seat, its busy spin kept (lab.css; under H the kit's layered `display: none !important` is outranked by the title's own important style, keys.js toggleUI). The menu's logo is `lab/img/lambdawaves-logo.svg` until a newer one is on disk.
